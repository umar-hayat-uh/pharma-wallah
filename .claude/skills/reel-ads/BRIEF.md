# PharmaWallah Reel Ads — the owner's design brief (verbatim, 2026-10-10)

> Kept word for word so a later session can see what was asked. Where it conflicts with the brand or
> the repo, `SKILL.md` §"Where this brief is overridden" says what wins and why. **Read that first.**

---

Act as a senior motion graphics designer, creative director, and video-generation engineer for PharmaWallah, a pharmacy education and technology platform.

Your task is to create premium, engaging, modern video advertisements for PharmaWallah's website, mobile app, and pharmacy calculators.

1. Visual Style

- Create cinematic, high-quality tech-product launch ads.
- Use modern motion graphics, 3D phone mockups, animated UI, kinetic typography, and smooth transitions.
- Brand colors: Blue "#2563EB", Green "#4ADE80", White "#FFFFFF", and Dark Navy "#0B1220".
- Use blue-to-green gradients, subtle glow effects, clean backgrounds, and premium lighting.
- Make every scene visually interesting, polished, and professionally composed.
- Use authentic PharmaWallah screenshots, logos, and app interfaces whenever available.
- Avoid generic stock footage, cluttered layouts, excessive particles, and cheap-looking transitions.

2. Reel Specifications

- Format: Vertical 9:16.
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS.
- Duration: 15–30 seconds by default.
- Optimize for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp Status.
- Keep text large, readable, and within mobile-safe areas.

3. Video Structure

Build the reel using this sequence:

Scene 1 — Hook (0–3 sec)
Grab attention immediately using an interesting visual, a relatable pharmacy-student problem, or a bold headline.

Scene 2 — Product Reveal (3–7 sec)
Introduce PharmaWallah with a smooth logo animation, app mockup, or website interface reveal.

Scene 3 — Feature Demonstration (7–16 sec)
Show actual product features through animated UI, calculator inputs, numerical results, graphs, or app interactions.

Scene 4 — Benefits (16–22 sec)
Communicate the practical value for pharmacy students using short, powerful text.

Scene 5 — Call to Action (22–30 sec)
Finish with a premium brand end card, clear CTA, and "pharmawallah.com" or the relevant app-download destination.

Adjust the timeline to suit the requested reel duration.

4. Animation Direction

Use:

- Smooth camera movements and cinematic zooms.
- Fast but readable kinetic typography.
- UI cards that slide, expand, and transform naturally.
- Animated graphs, calculation sequences, and number reveals.
- 3D smartphone rotations and screen transitions.
- Motion blur, subtle depth, layered compositions, and clean wipes.
- Beat-synchronized transitions and tasteful sound effects.

Every animation must serve the story. Avoid random movements, excessive bouncing, and repetitive transitions.

5. Typography and Copy

Use bold, modern sans-serif typography such as Inter or Manrope.

Keep copy short, punchy, and student-friendly. Each scene should communicate one idea.

Example hooks:

- "Still calculating everything manually?"
- "Your pharmacy practicals just got easier."
- "Meet your digital pharmacy toolkit."
- "All your essential calculations in one app."

Possible CTAs:

- "Get the PharmaWallah App"
- "Explore PharmaWallah"
- "Try the Calculators"
- "Visit pharmawallah.com"

Use only claims supported by the actual product. Mention offline functionality only when the advertised app version supports it.

6. Sound Design

Use energetic, modern technology-style background music with subtle digital clicks, whooshes, transitions, and impact sounds.

Synchronize important animations with the music. Ensure the reel remains understandable without sound through clear on-screen text.

Use only audio that is licensed or permitted for publication.

7. Technical Execution

Before creating the reel:

1. Inspect the project files and available assets.
2. Identify the available video-generation or animation tools.
3. Reuse existing dependencies where possible.
4. Create a scene-by-scene storyboard and implement the animation.
5. Render the video, inspect it, and fix visual or timing problems.
6. Export the final MP4 and retain editable source files.

Use Remotion, React, CSS/SVG animation, FFmpeg, or an already configured video-generation tool where appropriate. Do not assume external APIs or credentials are available.

Never claim the video was rendered successfully unless the actual output exists.

8. Product Accuracy

- Use real PharmaWallah branding and genuine product interfaces.
- Verify calculator values, formulas, units, and displayed results.
- Never invent features, testimonials, statistics, or medical claims.
- Do not distort the logo or create misleading interface demonstrations.
- Keep the design professional, trustworthy, and appropriate for pharmacy education.

Final Direction

The finished reel should look like a premium technology startup advertisement—not a basic slideshow.

Prioritize a powerful first three seconds, impressive motion graphics, authentic product demonstrations, polished transitions, readable text, engaging pacing, and a strong final call to action.

Brand: PharmaWallah
Website: https://pharmawallah.com
Audience: Pharm-D students and pharmacy learners
Goal: Create visually striking reels that attract attention, demonstrate real value, and encourage students to try PharmaWallah.
