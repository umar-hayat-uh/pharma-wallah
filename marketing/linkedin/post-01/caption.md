We were never short of study material. We were short of one place to put it.

PharmaWallah is a free study platform for Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm-D) students, built in Karachi for the syllabus students actually sit.

→ 105 pharmacy calculators, each showing its working so you can check yours
→ Lessons organised semester by semester, unit by unit, with MCQ banks for practice
→ Histology, pathology and powder microscopy slides, with timed identification tests
→ 8 virtual wet labs, so you can run the experiment before the practical
→ A drug encyclopedia covering 12,673 drug records

No account is needed for the tools, and the calculators are also available as a free, offline Android app.

This page is where we'll share new tools, worked formulas and what we're building next. Follow along, and if you teach or study pharmacy, we'd like to hear what would help you most.
